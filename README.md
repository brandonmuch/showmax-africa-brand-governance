## Responsibilities

### Cross-Border Information Governance
- Contributed to Showmax Africa's 12-country digital infrastructure rollout as part of the founding strategy team, upholding cross-border information security protocols throughout
- Monitored brand compliance across 48 social profiles across Africa, using audience metrics to identify reputational risk and guide strategic content pivots

### Brand Governance Framework Design
- Developed comprehensive brand guidelines covering logo placement, colour usage, typography, and image treatment across all 12 markets
- Documented compliant and non-compliant usage rules, giving market teams clear governance boundaries without requiring central approval on every output
- Created a reference system that standardised brand decisions across geographically dispersed teams operating in different regulatory and cultural contexts

### Scalable Asset Governance
- Designed responsive social covers and profile images for 1 markets, each adapted for local platform specifications while remaining within governed brand parameters
- Built reusable template systems allowing market teams to produce compliant assets independently and at scale
- Established format governance across all markets: standardised dimensions, file types, and naming conventions on Facebook, Instagram, and X simultaneously
